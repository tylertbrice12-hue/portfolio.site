About

I'm an AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner with hands-on experience across EC2, S3, CloudFront, CloudWatch, IAM, SNS, VPC, RDS, DynamoDB, and EFS — built through self-directed projects and structured labs, not coursework alone. On my monitoring project, I configured a CloudWatch alarm to trigger at 50% CPU utilization during a simulated workload spike, and confirmed the SNS notification landed in about 3 minutes. That's the kind of monitoring-to-alert pipeline I want to keep building.

Before cloud, I spent several years in healthcare operations, diagnosing and escalating technical system failures under pressure. I troubleshot EHR connectivity issues, triaged problems by severity, and documented fixes for teams who depended on the systems staying up. Moving into cloud operations felt less like starting over and more like applying the same instinct to different infrastructure.

I'm currently studying for the AWS Solutions Architect – Associate certification and building toward Cloud Support, Cloud Operations, DevOps Support, and Site Reliability roles.
